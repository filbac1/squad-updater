Squad Updater reads your database, takes current data from Transfermarkt and EA FC, and saves a new database next to the old one. Your database is never changed.

  1. Check the database at the top left (Change database if needed).
  2. On New update, choose players and changes.
  3. When it finishes, copy the new database into the game.
